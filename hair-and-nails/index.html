Hair and nails in one visit

Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa is a family-owned hair salon with a nail room of its own, at 4116 Grand Blvd in New Port Richey. Book your hair and your nails together and get it all done in one trip.

Text to bookCall (727) 487-4475

Hair, with our stylists

Haircuts for women, men and kids, from $25. Color, highlights, balayage and gray blending, from $60, and every color service starts with a free consultation. Perms, deep-conditioning and smoothing treatments, and updos for weddings, proms and quinceañeras.

Nails, with Kelly

Kelly has 33 years at the nail table. She does classic manicures, spa pedicures with a relaxing lower-leg massage, gel polish, acrylic nails, and chrome and cat-eye finishes, in a quiet nail room of her own right beside the styling floor. It's the day-spa side of Classic Styles: unhurried and meticulously clean.

How to book both

Call or text (727) 487-4475 and tell us what you'd like, for example “a cut and color, plus a spa pedicure.” We'll do our best to book them back to back. You can also text Kelly directly at (970) 590-9123 for nails. Walk-ins are welcome Tuesday to Saturday, but for hair and nails in the same visit, booking ahead is the surest way to get both.

Good to know

  • Open Tuesday to Friday 9 am to 5 pm, and Saturday 9 am to 3 pm.
  • Free parking on the side of the building; come in the front or back door.
  • Se habla español: ask for Aury.
  • Gift certificates work for any amount or a specific service, including a manicure or pedicure.

Questions

Can I book my hair and nails for the same visit?

Yes. Ask for both when you call or text (727) 487-4475, and we'll do our best to book them back to back.

Do you do acrylic nails?

Yes. Kelly does acrylic nails, gel polish, chrome and cat-eye finishes, and classic polish.

How much do nail services cost?

Nail prices depend on the service, so call or text for an exact price. Haircuts start at $25 and color at $60.
