Color Correction in New Port Richey

Brassy, banded, too dark or box-dyed? We plan the fix in steps that keep your hair healthy, and we tell you the price first.

Honesty first, miracles second

Almost everyone has had a color moment that did not go to plan. The box said light golden brown and your hair says midnight. The blonde went orange. The roots and ends do not match. It happens a lot, and it can usually be fixed.

We see clients from New Port Richey, Trinity, Odessa and across Pasco County for exactly this. Start with a photo and a free consultation by call or text.

How we plan a correction

We look first. At your free consultation we look at your hair in the light and ask about your color history. Bring a photo of the shade you want, and the box if it was a box dye.

We work in stages when needed. Moving from dark or uneven to light and even is gentler on your hair when it is not rushed, so a correction sometimes happens over more than one visit. We will tell you what we expect after we see your hair.

You get a price before we start. Color correction is quoted by consultation, because it depends on your hair and where it is starting from.

Not sure what to do before you come in? Read our advice on fixing box dye mistakes: what to do, what not to do, and what to bring.

Color correction questions, answered

How much does color correction cost?

Color correction is quoted by consultation, because it depends on your hair and where it is starting from. We give you a plan and a price before we begin. Regular color at Classic Styles starts from $60.

Can you fix box dye?

Often, yes. Please do not dye over it again or bleach it at home before you come in. Bring the box or a photo of it, plus a photo of the color you want, and we will plan the fix with you.

Will it take more than one visit?

Sometimes. A careful correction can happen in stages, which is gentler on your hair than rushing it. After we see your hair, we will tell you honestly how many steps we expect.
