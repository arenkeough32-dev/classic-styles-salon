VIP color consultation for color correction, blonding and big changes

Bigger color work starts with a plan. Answer five quick questions and send two photos. Tammy’s team looks at your hair history before you ever sit down, then texts you back with honest timing, what it will take and a starting price.

Free, no obligation. Family-owned and women-owned on Grand Boulevard for more than 35 years.

Five questions, about two minutes

When you press send, your phone opens a text to the salon with your answers already filled in. Attach your photos there and send. A real person reads every one.

1What are you hoping for?
2Your hair history, last 2 years

Be honest, nothing shocks us. Henna and some “natural” dyes react with lightener, so they matter most.

3Your hair today
4Your photos

After you press send, attach these in the text that opens (tap the photo or + button):

  1. Your hair right now, near a window in daylight, from the back and the front.
  2. One or two goal photos you love.

Photos are how we price fairly and plan the right amount of time. Without them we can only guess.
