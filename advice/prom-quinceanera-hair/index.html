Prom and Quinceañera Hair: When to Book and What to Bring

By Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa · November 8, 2026

Quick answer: Book prom hair as soon as you know the date, because spring Saturdays fill fast. For a quinceañera, book 1 to 3 months ahead and plan a trial run a few weeks before. Bring photos of the style you love, your dress color, and any tiara, crown or hair accessories you’ll wear.

Prom hair

Most prom styling happens on a handful of Saturdays in spring, so chairs go quickly. Text the date to the salon early. Come with clean, dry hair unless your stylist says otherwise, and wear a button-up shirt so you don’t pull anything over your finished style.

Quinceañera hair

A quinceañera style has to hold up through the ceremony, photos and a long night of dancing, often with a tiara. A trial lets you and your stylist test the style with the crown and make changes before the day. Aury on our team speaks Spanish, so families can plan in either language.

Groups: court, damas and moms

Tell us how many people need styling and what time you have to leave. We’ll build a schedule so everyone is ready on time.

What to bring

  • Two or three photos of styles you like
  • A photo of the dress, or the color
  • Your tiara, crown, clips or flowers
  • The time you need to be finished
