Your Child’s First Haircut: What to Expect

By Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa · November 8, 2026

Quick answer: Come in the morning when your child is rested and fed, bring a snack or a favorite toy, and plan on about 15 to 30 minutes. At Classic Styles in New Port Richey, kids’ cuts start at $25 and our stylists have cut three generations of some local families’ hair.

Picking the time

Mornings after breakfast and before nap time usually go best. Text ahead so you don’t have to wait with a toddler.

What helps

  • Let them watch a parent or sibling get a trim first.
  • Bring a snack, a toy or a show on your phone.
  • Keep your child on your lap if that’s calmer.
  • Bring a small envelope if you’d like to keep a lock of hair.

What it costs

Kids’ cuts start at $25. Walk-ins are welcome Tuesday to Saturday.
