How to Choose a Hair Salon in New Port Richey

By Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa · November 8, 2026

Quick answer: Look for a salon with many recent, detailed reviews, a free consultation for color, stylists with real experience in the service you want, and a price you hear before anything starts. In New Port Richey, Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa on Grand Blvd has been family-run for 25 years, has a 5.0 Google rating, and its stylists have 30 to 40 years behind the chair.

1. Read the reviews, not just the stars

A 5.0 rating from five reviews tells you less than a high rating from fifty. Read what people say about the service you want. Do color clients mention the result lasting? Do people say the price matched what they were told?

2. Ask for a consultation

For color, extensions or a big change, a good salon offers a consultation first. It’s where you find out if what you want is realistic for your hair, how many visits it takes, and what it costs.

3. Match the stylist to the job

Every stylist has strengths. Ask who on the team does the most color correction, balayage, perms or bridal hair. At Classic Styles, for example, Natalie focuses on color correction and blonding, Debbie on perms and classic cuts, and Tammy on color and bridal.

4. Hear the price first

You should know the price before the cape goes on. If a salon can’t give you at least a range, ask why.

5. Check the practical things

  • Do they take walk-ins, or only appointments?
  • Is there parking?
  • Can you text to book?
  • Does anyone speak your language? At Classic Styles, Aury speaks Spanish.

Questions to ask before you book

  1. Who would you put me with, and how long have they done this?
  2. What will it cost, and what could change the price?
  3. How long will it take, and how often will I need to come back?
  4. Can I see photos of similar work?
