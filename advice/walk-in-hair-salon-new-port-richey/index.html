Walk-In Hair Salon in New Port Richey: How It Works

By Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa · November 8, 2026

Quick answer: Classic Styles takes walk-ins Tuesday through Saturday at 4116 Grand Blvd in New Port Richey. Haircuts, trims, blowouts and many nail services work well as walk-ins. For color or anything bigger, text first, because those start with a consultation. To skip any wait, text (727) 487-4475 before you head over.

Best times to walk in

Weekday mornings are usually the quietest. Saturdays and the days before holidays are the busiest.

Good walk-in services

  • Haircuts for women, men and kids (from $25, about 30 minutes)
  • Trims and bang trims
  • Blowouts
  • Many manicures and pedicures

Better to text first

  • Color, highlights and balayage
  • Color correction
  • Perms
  • Bridal and event hair

Hours

Tuesday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm. Saturday, 9 am to 3 pm. Closed Sunday and Monday. Park on the side of the building and come in the front or back door (Unit 1).

Questions
