How Much Do Hand-Tied Extensions Cost?

By Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa · November 8, 2026

Quick answer: Hand-tied extensions are quoted after a consultation, because the price depends on how much hair you need, how long it is, and how many rows it takes to look natural on your head. Plan for the hair itself, the installation, and move-up appointments every 6 to 8 weeks. At Classic Styles in New Port Richey we quote it all in full before we begin.

What decides the price

  • How much hair: more fullness means more wefts.
  • Length: longer hair costs more.
  • Rows: fine hair may need more rows to hide the wefts.
  • Color match: blending two shades for a natural result.

The upkeep

As your hair grows, the rows move down. Every 6 to 8 weeks the stylist moves them back up. With good care, the hair can be reused for several move-ups.

Are they right for you?

Hand-tied extensions sit on a thin row of beads with no glue or heat, so they suit many hair types, but not every head of hair. We look first and tell you honestly.

How often do hand-tied extensions need maintenance?

Most people come in every 6 to 8 weeks for a move-up as their hair grows.

Do hand-tied extensions damage hair?

They use no glue or heat. Installed and maintained properly, they suit many hair types. A stylist should check your hair first.
