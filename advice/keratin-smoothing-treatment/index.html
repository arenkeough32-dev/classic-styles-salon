Keratin Smoothing Treatments: What to Expect

By Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa · November 9, 2026

Quick answer: A keratin-style smoothing treatment seals the outside of each strand so it soaks up less moisture from the air. Your hair frizzes less, blow-dries faster and stays smoother for weeks. At Classic Styles on Grand Blvd it starts with a free consultation and is priced by your hair’s length, thickness and condition, so call or text (727) 487-4475 for an exact quote.

What it does, and what it doesn’t

Smoothing is made for frizz. It calms the rough, lifted outer layer of the hair that swells in Gulf Coast humidity, which is why it works so well here. Hair looks shinier, tangles less and takes less time to dry and style.

It is not a relaxer. It does not permanently change your curl pattern, and it fades gradually as you wash. Many people find it loosens wave a little; if you love your curls and only want less frizz, tell us and we’ll plan for that. You may hear it called a keratin treatment, a smoothing treatment or by a brand name. At your consultation we’ll tell you exactly what we use and why.

Who it suits

  • Hair that puffs up the minute you walk outside in summer
  • Thick or coarse hair that takes a long time to blow-dry
  • Color-treated hair that has turned dry and frizzy
  • Anyone tired of fighting their hair every morning

If you’re pregnant or sensitive to strong smells, mention it when you book so we can talk through the options first.

How long it lasts

Most clients get several weeks of smoother hair. It wears off slowly instead of stopping all at once. Washing less often, using a sulfate-free shampoo and rinsing your hair before you swim all help it last longer.

Aftercare

Your stylist will tell you how long to wait before washing, tying your hair back or swimming, because that depends on the formula. After that, keep hot tools on a moderate setting and use a light leave-in on damp hair. Our guide to smooth hair in Florida humidity has the full routine.

Smoothing or a deep-conditioning treatment?

A deep-conditioning treatment is a quick moisture reset and an easy add-on to any cut or color. Smoothing lasts longer and is aimed squarely at frizz. If you’re not sure which your hair needs, we’ll tell you honestly, not just the most expensive one.

Planning it with color

If you’re also coloring or lightening, tell us at the consultation. We’ll plan the order of the two services so both last as long as possible.

Questions

How long does a keratin smoothing treatment last?

Most clients get several weeks of smoother, less frizzy hair, and it fades gradually rather than all at once. How long yours lasts depends on your hair, how often you wash and whether you swim.

Will keratin smoothing straighten curly hair?

It smooths frizz and can loosen wave a little, but it is not a relaxer and does not permanently change your curl. Tell us at the consultation how much curl you want to keep.
