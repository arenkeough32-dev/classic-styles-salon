How Much Does Balayage Cost in New Port Richey?

By Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa · November 8, 2026

Quick answer: At Classic Styles in New Port Richey, color, highlights and balayage start at $60. Your exact price depends on your hair’s length and thickness, your starting color, and how light you want to go. Text one photo of your hair and one of the look you want to (727) 487-4475 and we’ll give you an honest estimate, then confirm the price at a free consultation before anything starts.

What balayage is

Balayage is hand-painted color. Instead of foils in a strict pattern, the stylist paints lighter pieces where the sun would naturally hit. It grows out softly, so most people can go longer between appointments than with traditional highlights.

What changes the price

  • Length and thickness. Long, thick hair takes more product and more time.
  • Your starting color. Going from dark brown to bright blonde is more work than a soft brightening.
  • Old color in your hair. Box dye or several past colors may need a correction step first. See our color correction cost guide.
  • Toner or gloss. Most balayage finishes with a toner so the blonde looks creamy, not brassy.
  • A haircut or treatment. Many clients add a trim or a deep-conditioning treatment for Florida sun and salt.

Why we quote first

Nobody likes a surprise at the register. Every color service at Classic Styles starts with a free consultation. We look at your hair in the light, talk about what is realistic, and tell you the price before we begin. If you’re not sure, try colors on yourself first with our live color mirror.

How long it lasts

Because balayage grows out softly, many clients come back every 8 to 12 weeks for a gloss or touch-up, and every few months for a full refresh. Your stylist will tell you what fits your hair and your budget.
