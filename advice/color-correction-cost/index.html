How Much Does Color Correction Cost?

By Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa · November 8, 2026

Quick answer: Color correction is priced by consultation because no two corrections are the same. The cost depends on what is in your hair now, how far it is from your goal, your hair’s length and condition, and whether it is safest to do in more than one visit. At Classic Styles in New Port Richey the consultation is free, and you get a written plan and price before we start.

What counts as color correction

  • Box dye that came out too dark, orange or patchy
  • Brassy or yellow blonde
  • Banding, where roots, middle and ends are different colors
  • Going back to your natural shade after years of coloring
  • A previous salon result that missed the mark

What affects the price

  • How many layers of old color you have. Each one has to be dealt with.
  • How big the change is. Dark to light takes far more work than warm to cool.
  • Length and thickness. More hair means more time and product.
  • Hair health. If your hair needs a rest, we split the work into stages with conditioning in between.

Why it can take more than one visit

Lifting old color out and putting the right tone back in is a lot to ask of your hair in one sitting. Doing it in stages keeps your hair soft and strong. We tell you honestly at the start whether it’s likely to be one visit or a few, so you can plan for it.

How to get a price

  1. Text a photo of your hair now, and one of the color you want, to (727) 487-4475.
  2. Tell us what has been on your hair in the last two years: box dye, salon color, henna, a perm or a relaxer.
  3. Come in for a free consultation. We check your hair in the light and give you the plan and the price.

Natalie on our team specializes in color correction and blonding. Read more on our color correction page, or see what to do after a box-dye mistake.

Questions

How much does color correction cost in New Port Richey?

Color correction at Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa is priced after a free consultation, because it depends on your current color, your goal, your hair’s length and its condition. You get the plan and price before anything starts.

How many visits does color correction take?

Sometimes one, sometimes a few. Big changes, like dark box dye to blonde, are safer in stages. We tell you honestly at the consultation.

Can I fix box dye at home?

We don’t recommend it. Another box or home bleach usually makes the correction longer. Text us a photo first at (727) 487-4475.

Want an exact price?

Text one photo of your hair and one of the look you want to (727) 487-4475. We’ll give you an honest answer, then a price before we start.
