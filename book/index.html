Book your appointment

The quickest way is a text. Tap the button, tell us what you’d like and when, and the team texts you back with a time. Most texts get an answer the same day we’re open.

Text to bookCall (727) 487-4475

Open Tuesday to Friday 9 to 5, Saturday 9 to 3. Button not working? Text (727) 487-4475.

What to put in your text

  1. Your name, and whether you’re new to the salon.
  2. The service: a cut, color, highlights, balayage, a perm, a treatment, or nails.
  3. Your stylist, if you have a favorite.
  4. The days and times that work for you.
  5. For color, a photo of your hair now and one of the look you want, so we can give you an honest price before anything starts.

Color, highlights and balayage start at $60, and haircuts at $25. The exact price is always given before we begin.

Good to know

Planning color correction, blonding or a big change?

Start with a free VIP color consultation: five quick questions and two photos, and we text you a plan and a starting price.

Can I just walk in?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome Tuesday to Saturday for haircuts, trims, blowouts and many nail services. Text ahead and we’ll tell you how long the wait is. More in our walk-in guide.

How far ahead should I book?

A few days ahead is usually plenty. Saturdays fill fast, so book those early. For weddings, proms and quinceañeras, see our weddings page.

Running late or need to change a time?

Text (727) 487-4475 as soon as you can. The team checks texts between clients.

Where do I park?

We’re at 4116 Grand Blvd, Unit 1, the white former bank building on the corner of Grand Blvd and Olympia Street. Park on the side of the building and come in the front or back door.
