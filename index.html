Questions, answered

Straight answers to what people ask us most. See all twelve on our hair questions page.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome Tuesday through Saturday. To guarantee a chair, call or text (727) 487-4475 before you come in; same-day appointments are often available.

How do I book an appointment?

The fastest way to book is to call or text (727) 487-4475. There is no online booking system. A real person answers.

Is Classic Styles good for kids?

Absolutely. Children’s haircuts start at $25, our stylists are patient with first haircuts and wiggly toddlers, and families have been bringing their kids here for generations.

Where do I park?

Parking is available on the side of the building at 4116 Grand Blvd, Unit 1, New Port Richey, FL 34652, no meters, no garage, just pull in and walk a few steps to the door.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards and tap-to-pay (Apple Pay, Google Pay and contactless cards), as well as cash.

What are your hours?

Tuesday through Friday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. We are closed Sunday and Monday.

How much does a haircut cost in New Port Richey?

Most haircuts at Classic Styles start at $25 for women, men and kids. The final price depends on length and thickness, and we always quote before we start. Walk-ins are welcome, or text (727) 487-4475 to hear the current wait.

Balayage vs highlights: which is better?

Neither is better: they’re different tools: highlights give precise, all-over brightness, while balayage is hand-painted and grows out softly with fewer touch-ups. In sunny Florida, balayage is especially forgiving between visits.

How do I stop my hair from frizzing in Florida humidity?

Humidity only frizzes hair that’s dry and porous, because moisture sneaks into the shaft and swells it. The fix we do most is a humidity-shield conditioning treatment paired with the right cut for your natural texture.

How long does a perm last?

Most perms hold their shape for 3 to 6 months, depending on your hair and how you care for it. Modern perms are far gentler than the ’80s version, a free consult tells you if your hair is a good candidate.
