Good to Know

Questions, Answered

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome Tuesday through Saturday. Because stylists may be with clients, the fastest way to guarantee a chair is to call or text (727) 487-4475 before you come in. Same-day appointments are often available.

How much is a haircut?

Haircuts start at $25 for women, men and children. Color and highlights start at $40. Perms, nails, facials and bridal styling are quoted individually — call or text (727) 487-4475 for an exact quote in under a minute.

Where are you located?

We are at 4116 Grand Blvd, New Port Richey, FL 34652, with free parking right outside. We serve clients from New Port Richey, Port Richey, Holiday, Hudson and the surrounding Gulf Coast.

Do you do bridal and wedding hair?

Yes — bridal updos and special-occasion styling are a house specialty, and our team can accommodate full wedding parties. Call or text (727) 487-4475 to plan your date; booking a few weeks ahead is recommended for groups.

What are your hours?

Tuesday through Friday 9 AM to 5 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 3 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday. Holiday hours are posted on our Google Business Profile.
