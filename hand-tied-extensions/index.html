Extensions that start with a conversation

If you want more length, more fullness, or both, hand-tied extensions are one way to get there. At Classic Styles we do not guess from a menu. We look at your hair first, talk through what you want, and then quote it in full before anything begins.

Whether you are in New Port Richey, Trinity, Odessa or anywhere else in Pasco County, the first step is the same: a free consultation by call or text.

How it works at Classic Styles

1. Free consultation. Tell us the length and fullness you are after. Bring a photo if you have one. We will look at your hair and talk honestly about what is realistic.

2. A color match. Extensions are matched to your color and placed by hand, so they blend with the hair you already have. If your color needs a refresh first, our stylists can plan that with you in the same conversation.

3. A full quote before we start. Hand-tied extensions are priced by consultation. We quote the whole service up front, so there are no surprises at the chair.

Stylists with decades behind the chair

Owner and master stylist Tammy has more than 35 years behind the chair, and Debbie has 41. Color is part of nearly everything we do, from lived-in balayage and blonde to color correction, which helps when your extensions need to match a specific shade.

Ready to talk it through? Call or text the salon at (727) 487-4475. We are at 4116 Grand Blvd, Unit 1, open Tuesday to Friday 9 am to 5 pm and Saturday 9 am to 3 pm.

Extension questions, answered

How much do hand-tied extensions cost at Classic Styles?

Hand-tied extensions are priced by consultation. We look at your hair first, then quote the service in full before we start. Call or text (727) 487-4475 to set up a free consultation.

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. We look at your hair, talk about the length and fullness you want, and match the extensions to your color. The consultation is free.

Can you match extensions to my current color?

Yes, matching to your color is part of the service. If your color needs work first, our stylists can plan that with you at your consultation.
