Our story, our team, and quick facts for press

In short: Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa is a family-owned, women-owned hair salon and nail salon at 4116 Grand Blvd, Unit 1, in New Port Richey, Florida. It has been on Grand Blvd for 25 years. Owner and master stylist Tammy has more than 35 years behind the chair, and the team of six includes stylists with up to 41 years of experience and a Spanish-speaking stylist.

Our story

Classic Styles is a neighborhood salon in the white former First State Bank building on the corner of Grand Blvd and Olympia St. For 25 years, clients from New Port Richey, Holiday, Trinity, Port Richey, Hudson and Odessa have come here for cuts, color, perms and wedding hair, often bringing their kids and grandkids along.

The idea has stayed simple: a real person answers the phone, every color starts with a free consultation, and you hear the price before anything begins.

The team

  • Tammy, owner and master stylist. More than 35 years behind the chair: color, cuts, perms and bridal.
  • Debbie, stylist, 41 years. Perms, color, and cuts for women, men and children.
  • Natalie, stylist. Color correction, blonding, vivid color and bridal updos.
  • Aury, stylist. Cuts, color and styling in English or Spanish.
  • Jen, stylist. Cuts, color and styling.
  • Kelly, nail technician, 33 years. Manicures, pedicures, gel and nail art.
