It starts with a real consultation

Every cut at Classic Styles begins with a conversation, not clippers. Your stylist will ask how you style your hair on a normal Tuesday — not just your best day — and factor in face shape, cowlicks, thickness and how much time you honestly want to spend on it.

Bring a photo if you have one. We’ll tell you straight whether it suits your hair type, what we’d adjust, and how to recreate the finish at home with the products you already own.

That honesty is why clients stay for decades — and why a $25 cut here outperforms a $60 one elsewhere. Pair your cut with color or highlights for the full transformation.

Haircut questions, answered

Do you take walk-ins for haircuts?

Yes — walk-ins are welcome Tuesday through Saturday. To guarantee a chair, call or text (727) 487-4475 before you come in; same-day appointments are often available.

How much is a haircut at Classic Styles?

Women’s, men’s and children’s haircuts all start at $25, including a style consultation. Most cuts take about 30 minutes.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Absolutely — children’s cuts start at $25, and our stylists are patient with first haircuts and wiggly toddlers. Families have trusted us for generations.
