The full day-spa finish, under one roof

Classic Styles started as a hair salon in 1990 and grew into a full day spa — and nails are where the “spa” really shows. Our pedicure chairs are unhurried, our stations are spotless, and you’re never double-booked against three other clients.

Because we do hair too, we’re the easy answer for wedding mornings, proms and big events: cuts and updos, color and nails in one visit, with free parking right at the door on Grand Blvd.

Regulars pair a spa pedicure every few weeks with a deep-conditioning treatment — an hour that resets hair, hands and mood at once.

Nail care questions, answered

How long does a spa pedicure take?

Plan on 45 to 60 minutes for a proper spa pedicure — soak, shaping, cuticle care, exfoliation and a relaxing massage. Manicures usually take about 30 minutes. Add them to a haircut or color appointment and make an afternoon of it.

Do you offer gel polish?

Yes — gel manicures and pedicures in a range of colors, cured for a chip-free finish that typically lasts two to three weeks even with Florida pool and beach days.

Can I book nail services for a wedding or event party?

Absolutely — bridal parties and groups are welcome. Book a few weeks ahead for groups so we can reserve enough chairs, and ask about pairing nails with updos and styling for the full day-spa finish.
