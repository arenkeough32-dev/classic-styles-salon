Plan your visit

Everything to know before you come to Classic Styles on Grand Blvd, from where to park to how to get in.

Finding us

4116 Grand Blvd, Unit 1, New Port Richey, FL 34652. We're in the white former First State Bank building on the corner of Grand Blvd and Olympia St, just north of SR 54.

Get directions

Parking and doors

Park for free in the lot on the side of the building. You can come in the front door or the back door.

Accessibility

We have wheelchair-accessible parking, an accessible entrance, accessible seating and an accessible restroom. If you're coming with someone who needs a hand, or you'd like a quieter time, tell us when you book. Weekday mornings are usually the quietest; Saturdays and the days before holidays are the busiest.

Hours

Tuesday to Friday: 9 am to 5 pm. Saturday: 9 am to 3 pm. Sunday and Monday: closed. Around holidays, call or text to check.

Walk-ins and appointments

Walk-ins are welcome. For color, perms, bridal hair and nails, booking ahead is best so we can set aside the right amount of time.

Paying

We accept all major credit cards and tap-to-pay (Apple Pay, Google Pay and contactless cards), as well as cash.

Who's welcome

Women, men and kids, first haircuts included. Se habla español: Aury does cuts, color and styling in English or Spanish.

Questions before you come?

Call or text (727) 487-4475. A real person at the salon reads every text.
