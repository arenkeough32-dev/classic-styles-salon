Color formulated for Florida humidity

New Port Richey air is hard on color. Humidity swells the hair shaft and lets pigment escape; sun and chlorine bleach it out faster still. Color that looks perfect in a northern salon can fade brassy here in weeks.

Our stylists formulate for it: professional-grade color lines chosen for Gulf Coast conditions, cooler base tones to fight brass, and finishing products that seal the cuticle against moisture. We’ll also map a home-care routine so your color lasts the full stretch between appointments.

Pair any color service with a deep-conditioning treatment to lock in shine — it’s the single best defense against Florida frizz.

Color questions, answered

How much does hair coloring cost at Classic Styles?

Color and highlights start at $60. Balayage and more complex work are quoted after a free consultation — call or text (727) 487-4475 for an exact price before you book.

Will my color hold up in Florida humidity?

Yes. Our stylists use professional-grade color and finishing products chosen for Gulf Coast humidity, and we recommend a deep-conditioning treatment to lock in shine between visits.

Do I need a consultation before balayage?

We recommend one. Bring a photo of the look you want, and your stylist will map out placement, timing and cost — consultations are free and take just a few minutes.
