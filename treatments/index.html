Why Florida hair needs extra care

Living in Pasco County means year-round humidity, intense UV and — for most of us — pool chlorine and salt air. Each one lifts the hair cuticle; together they leave hair frizzy, brittle and dull no matter how good the cut underneath is.

Our stylists have treated Gulf Coast hair for over three decades. We match the treatment to the damage: moisture masks for dryness, protein for breakage, smoothing for frizz — and we’ll tell you honestly which one your hair actually needs, not the most expensive one.

Most clients pair a treatment with their color appointment every 4 to 6 weeks, or add one to a regular cut as a seasonal reset.

Treatment questions, answered

What hair treatments do you offer?

Deep-conditioning, smoothing and restorative treatments that repair sun, chlorine and humidity damage. Call or text (727) 487-4475 for pricing — most treatments are quoted by hair length and condition.

How often should I get a deep-conditioning treatment?

For Florida hair, every 4 to 6 weeks keeps moisture balanced — or add one to any color appointment to protect your investment.

Can I add a treatment to my cut or color?

Yes — treatments are our most popular add-on and usually take 15 to 20 minutes extra. Mention it when you call or text and we will book the time.
