Local Know-How

Perm expertise that’s getting rare

Most salons stopped training stylists on perms a decade ago — which is exactly why so many perms go wrong. Our stylists have been wrapping rods since before it was retro. Body waves, spiral wraps, partial perms for crown volume: we size the rods to your hair length and density, and we time the solution to your hair’s history, not a stopwatch.

Florida adds its own wrinkle: sun, salt and chlorine leave hair porous, and porous hair processes fast. We account for it, strand test when in doubt, and we’d rather talk you into a deep-conditioning treatment first than give you a frizzy perm you’ll hate.

Pair your perm with a precision cut to shape the curl, or add a moisture treatment to keep it soft through beach season.

Good to Know

Perm questions, answered

How long does a perm last?+

Most perms hold their shape for 3 to 6 months. Fine, straight hair relaxes a little faster; coarse or previously permed hair holds curl longer. We’ll tell you honestly at your consult what to expect from your hair type.

Can I get a perm if my hair is color-treated?+

Often, yes — if your hair is healthy. Perm solution works differently on lightened or heavily highlighted hair, so we always assess your hair’s condition first. If it needs strengthening, we’ll do a restorative treatment before we roll a single rod. Call or text (727) 487-4475 and we’ll take a look.

How do I care for a perm in Florida humidity?+

Wait at least 48 hours before washing, then use sulfate-free products and a light curl cream. The good news: Gulf Coast humidity is a perm’s friend — moisture keeps curls bouncy. We’ll send you home with a simple routine matched to your curl.
