Privacy Policy

The short version: this site runs no analytics and sets no cookies of its own — just information about our salon, a phone number to reach us, and two Google embeds explained below.

Call or Text (727) 487-4475 Back to homepage

What this site collects

Nothing by us. Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa’s website does not use cookies of its own, analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting, or any other form of tracking — the only external services are the two Google embeds described below. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about visitors.

Forms and accounts

This site has no contact forms, no booking accounts, no newsletters, and no comment sections — so there is no form data to collect. Everything on these pages is read-only information about our services, prices, and reviews.

Calling or texting us

If you reach us by phone or text at (727) 487-4475, that conversation stays between you and the salon. We use your number only to answer you and book your appointment — never for marketing lists, and we never sell or share it.

The two third parties: Google Fonts and Google Maps

Two parts of this site load content from Google. Our fonts are fetched from Google Fonts, which — like any font host — receives the standard technical request your browser makes when downloading font files (your IP address and browser type). The homepage also embeds a Google Maps frame showing our location, which loads directly from Google when you scroll to it. Both are governed by Google’s own privacy policy, and we do not use either to identify or track you.

Other than those two embedded services, this site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, and places no tracking pixels. We never see, store, or share anything about your visit.
