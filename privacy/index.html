Privacy Policy

The short version: this website collects nothing about you. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking — just information about our salon and a phone number to reach us.

What this site collects

Nothing. Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa’s website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting, or any other form of tracking. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about visitors.

Forms and accounts

This site has no contact forms, no booking accounts, no newsletters, and no comment sections — so there is no form data to collect. Everything on these pages is read-only information about our services, prices, and reviews.

Calling or texting us

If you reach us by phone or text at (727) 487-4475, that conversation stays between you and the salon. We use your number only to answer you and book your appointment — never for marketing lists, and we never sell or share it.

The one third party: Google Maps

Our pages link out to our Google Business Profile for directions and reviews. When you follow that link, you are on Google’s site, and Google’s own privacy policy applies from that point on. Until you click, nothing on our site talks to Google about you.
