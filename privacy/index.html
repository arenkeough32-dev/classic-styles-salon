Privacy Policy

What this site collects

Nothing by us. Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa’s website does not use cookies of its own, analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting, or any other form of tracking — the only outside services are the ones described below. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about visitors.

Forms and accounts

This site has no booking accounts, no newsletters and no comment sections. The VIP color consultation page has five questions, but it sends nothing to us or anyone else: it only fills in a text message on your own phone, which you choose whether to send. Everything on these pages is read-only information about our services, prices, and reviews.

Calling or texting us

If you reach us by phone or text at (727) 487-4475, that conversation stays between you and the salon. We use your number only to answer you and book your appointment — never for marketing lists, and we never sell or share it.

The outside services: Google Maps and a few on-page tools

Our fonts, images and animation scripts are hosted on this website itself, so loading a page does not contact Google Fonts or any other font service. The homepage embeds a Google Maps frame showing our location, and a Google Street View of our building if you choose to open it; both load directly from Google. On the Try a color page, your browser downloads a hair-detection model from Google (storage.googleapis.com) and its code from jsDelivr, and the 3D door plaque on the homepage loads its viewer from jsDelivr. The camera image on the try-on page is processed on your own device and never uploaded. These services follow their own privacy policies, including Google’s, and we do not use any of them to identify or track you.

Other than those two embedded services, this site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, and places no tracking pixels. We never see, store, or share anything about your visit.
