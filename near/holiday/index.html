A hair salon 10 minutes from Holiday, FL

Quick answer: Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa is a family-owned salon at 4116 Grand Blvd, Unit 1, New Port Richey, about 3 miles and 10 minutes north of Holiday. Haircuts start at $25 and color at $60, we take walk-ins Tuesday to Saturday, and Aury speaks Spanish.

The drive from Holiday

Take US-19 north to State Road 54, turn right and go about a mile east, then turn left onto Grand Blvd. We’re right there on the corner of Olympia St, in the white former First State Bank building. Park on the side of the building and use the front or back door (Unit 1). Get directions from Holiday.

Close enough to be your regular salon

Ten minutes away means you don’t have to save us for special occasions. These work well as walk-ins:

  • Haircuts for women, men and kids, from $25. Most take about 30 minutes.
  • Trims, bang trims and blowouts.
  • Many manicures and pedicures with Kelly, who has 33 years at the nail table.

Weekday mornings are usually the quietest. Saturdays are the busiest, so text (727) 487-4475 first and we’ll tell you the wait.

Kids’ cuts and first haircuts

Kids’ cuts start at $25 and first haircuts are welcome. Our stylists are patient with little ones who can’t sit still. Read our guide to a child’s first haircut before you come.

Hours

Tuesday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm, and Saturday, 9 am to 3 pm. Closed Sunday and Monday.
