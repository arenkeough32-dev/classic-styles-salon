Your neighborhood hair salon in Elfers, FL

Quick answer: Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa is a family-owned hair salon at 4116 Grand Blvd, Unit 1, in Elfers. Our mailing address says New Port Richey, so you may see both names. It’s the same shop. Haircuts start at $25 and color and balayage at $60, with color correction, perms, bridal hair and nails. Walk-ins are welcome Tuesday to Saturday.

Finding us

Look for the white former First State Bank building on the corner of Grand Blvd and Olympia St. Park on the side of the building and come in the front or back door (Unit 1). Get directions.

Close enough for quick visits

Being this close makes the small appointments easy: a walk-in trim or blowout, a haircut for the kids, or a fresh set of nails. For color or anything bigger, text first, because those start with a free consultation and you hear the price before anything begins. Tammy has more than 35 years at the chair and Debbie has 41, and we have been on Grand Blvd for 25 years.

What we do

  • Haircuts and styling for women, men and kids, from $25
  • Color and balayage, from $60, with a free consultation
  • Color correction for box dye, brassy or banded color
  • Perms and hair treatments
  • Nails: gel manicures and spa pedicures
  • Bridal, prom and quinceañera hair
