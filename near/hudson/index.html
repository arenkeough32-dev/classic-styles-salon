Home/Near Hudson

A family hair salon for Hudson, FL, 20 minutes down US-19

Quick answer: Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa is a family-owned salon at 4116 Grand Blvd, Unit 1, New Port Richey, about 12 miles south of Hudson (20 to 25 minutes). Haircuts start at $25 and color at $60, and you can book hair and nails in the same visit.

Getting here from Hudson

Take US-19 south for about 8 miles into New Port Richey. Turn left on Main Street, then right onto Grand Blvd, and follow it about 2 miles south to Olympia St. If you’d rather skip downtown, stay on US-19 to State Road 54, turn left, then left again onto Grand Blvd. We’re on the corner in the white former First State Bank building, with parking on the side. Get directions from Hudson.

Make one trip count

From Hudson, it makes sense to get everything done in one visit. Pair a cut or color with a manicure or pedicure in Kelly’s nail room, and tell us when you book so we can line up the times. For color, send photos first through the free VIP color consultation, so the plan and the price are set before you come.

Weddings, proms and quinceañeras

We style brides, bridesmaids and prom and quinceañera groups, in the salon or on location. Book a few weeks ahead, and plan a trial run for the bride. See our wedding hair.
