Home/Near Trinity

Hair color and cuts near Trinity, FL

Quick answer: Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa is a family-owned salon at 4116 Grand Blvd, Unit 1, New Port Richey, about 7 miles west of Trinity (15 to 20 minutes on SR 54). Color, highlights and balayage start at $60, haircuts at $25, and every color begins with a free consultation.

The drive from Trinity

From Trinity Blvd, take Little Road north to State Road 54 and turn left. Stay on SR 54 west for about 4 miles, then turn right onto Grand Blvd. We’re right on the corner of Olympia St, in the white former First State Bank building, with parking on the side. Get directions from Trinity.

Plan your color before you get in the car

Color takes the longest in the chair, so it pays to plan it first. Our free VIP color consultation asks five quick questions and two photos, and we text back a plan and a starting price. You know the time and the cost before you drive over.

The color we do

  • Balayage and lived-in blonde from $60, softened at the root so it grows out without a hard line, which means fewer trips back.
  • Gray blending: fine highlights and lowlights so gray grows in softly instead of a hard root line every few weeks.
  • Color correction for box dye, brassy or banded color, planned in steps and priced after a consultation.
  • Full color, highlights and root touch-ups from $60.

Owner Tammy has more than 35 years behind the chair, and Debbie has 41.

Weekdays 9 am to 5 pm, Saturdays 9 am to 3 pm, closed Sunday and Monday.
