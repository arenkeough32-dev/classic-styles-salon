A family hair salon near Port Richey, FL

Quick answer: Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa is a family-owned hair salon at 4116 Grand Blvd, Unit 1, New Port Richey, about 10 to 15 minutes from Port Richey. We do haircuts from $25, color and balayage from $60, color correction, perms, hand-tied extensions, bridal hair, and manicures and pedicures. Walk-ins are welcome Tuesday to Saturday, and Aury speaks Spanish.

Getting here from Port Richey

We are just north of us, a short drive down US-19. Look for the white former First State Bank building on the corner of Grand Blvd and Olympia St. Park on the side of the building and come in the front or back door (Unit 1). Get directions from Port Richey.

Why Port Richey clients drive in

Port Richey clients come for walk-in haircuts, perms and color, and for a salon where the price is said out loud before anything starts. Owner Tammy has more than 35 years behind the chair, Debbie has 41, and Kelly has done nails for 33. We have been on Grand Blvd for 25 years and every Google review is five stars.

What we do

  • Haircuts and styling for women, men and kids, from $25. Most cuts take about 30 minutes.
  • Color, highlights and balayage from $60, always with a free consultation and a price before we start.
  • Color correction for box dye, brassy or banded color, planned in safe steps.
  • Perms and body waves, a Classic Styles specialty.
  • Hand-tied extensions, quoted in full after a consultation.
  • Bridal and event hair for weddings, quinceañeras and proms.
  • Manicures and pedicures with Kelly in our own nail room.
