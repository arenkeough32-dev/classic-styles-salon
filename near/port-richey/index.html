A hair salon near Port Richey, FL, straight down Grand Blvd

Quick answer: Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa is a family-owned salon at 4116 Grand Blvd, Unit 1, New Port Richey, about 4 miles south of Port Richey (10 to 15 minutes). Perms and body waves are a Classic Styles specialty, and we also do cuts from $25, color from $60, smoothing treatments and nails.

Getting here from Port Richey

Head into downtown New Port Richey and pick up Grand Blvd. Follow it south for about 2 miles, past downtown, to Olympia St, just before State Road 54. We’re in the white former First State Bank building on the corner. Park on the side and come in the front or back door (Unit 1). Get directions from Port Richey.

Perms and body waves

We do classic perms, body waves, spiral curls and partial perms, from stylists with decades of perm experience. Most perms last 3 to 6 months. The price is set at a free consultation, because the right wave depends on your hair’s length, thickness and history. How long does a perm last?

Hair that holds up near the Gulf

Close to the water, humidity is a fact of life. A keratin-style smoothing treatment tames frizz for weeks, and deep conditioning brings back dry, sun-tired hair. We’ll tell you honestly whether your hair needs smoothing, moisture or protein before we start. See our treatments.

When we’re open

Tuesday through Friday 9 am to 5 pm, Saturday 9 am to 3 pm. We’re closed on Sunday and Monday. Call or text (727) 487-4475 to book.
