Wedding hair questions

How far ahead should we book wedding hair?

A few weeks ahead is best, especially for a whole wedding party. Text us your date and how many people need styling, and we’ll tell you what’s open.

Do you do hair for the whole wedding party?

Yes. Whole wedding parties are welcome: the bride, bridesmaids, mothers of the bride, and simple styles for flower girls.

Can you style us at our venue or bridal suite?

Ask about on-location styling. Miss Tammy styled the Tolliver wedding party in the bridal suite, and we’re happy to talk through what your day needs.

Can I do a trial run before the wedding?

Ask about a trial run when you text or call. It’s a good way to settle on your look before the big day.

Do you do quinceañera and prom hair?

Yes. We style quinceañeras and proms as well as weddings. Aury speaks Spanish, so ask for her if you’d like to plan in Spanish.

How much does bridal hair cost?

Bridal and special occasion hair is by consultation, so the price depends on the look and the number of people. Text or call (727) 487-4475 and we’ll go over it with you.
